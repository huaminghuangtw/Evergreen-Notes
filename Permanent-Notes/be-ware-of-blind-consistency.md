# Context > Consistency

* **Blind consistency** is just as bad as inconsistency. Inputs that fail to produce desired outcomes **should be adjusted, not repeated**.
* **[Adaptive](adaptability.md) consistency** is the key that unlocks your dream life.

---

The squirrel 🐿️ is what adaptive consistency looks like in motion: **a steady heading plus constant small corrections**. It adjusts the next step instead of repeating the last one.

* **Rough aim beats an exact plan** — You need a _direction_, not a map. The route is discovered in the running, not before it.
* **The stop is part of the run** — Looking around isn’t lost momentum; it’s where new input enters. Movement without looking is blind consistency at speed.
* **Correct the next step, not the destination** — Adjust the heading from what you just saw. You change the route, not the goal.

---

The definition of _insanity_ is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results.

> [!IMPORTANT] Variety + Consistency = Success
