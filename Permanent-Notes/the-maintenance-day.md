---

Group all of your “boring maintenance tasks” together (aka **batching**) and do them on this day.

---

# What Belongs on Maintenance Day

Any task that is necessary but not meaningful on its own, including:

* Paying bills and reviewing finances
* Grocery shopping and meal prepping
* Laundry, cleaning, and home organization
* Personal grooming (haircuts, nail care, etc.)
* Digital declutter (downloads folder, inbox zero)
* [Reviewing and responding to administrative emails](batching-emails-and-text-messages.md)
* Planning workouts and schedules for the coming week

The goal is to **clear the decks** so the rest of the week can be dedicated to creation, connection, and deep work.

# Tips

* **Batch sequentially, not in parallel.** [Do one task at a time](you-can-achieve-anything-if-you-focus-on-one-thing-at-a-time.md) and finish it before moving on. The point is to reduce mental load, not to rush.
* **[Pair it with something enjoyable.](temptation-bundling.md)** Listen to a podcast, audiobook, or music you love while you work.
* **Protect the other days.** When a maintenance impulse arises mid-week, write it down and let it wait until the appointed day. Trust the system.
