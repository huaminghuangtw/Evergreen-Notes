# Eliminate → Automate → Delegate

> “The most common error of a smart engineer is to optimize something that shouldn’t exist.” — Elon Musk

* Never automate something that can be eliminated.
* Never delegate something that can be automated or streamlined.

Every task on your plate should first be challenged: _Does this need to exist at all?_

# How to delegate tasks that are hard to do

Some tasks require judgment, [tacit knowledge](explicit-knowledge-and-tacit-knowledge.md), or contextual awareness that cannot be fully captured in a written process. For these, you need structured hand-off methods.

## Paired Tasking

### Paired tasking is best when the work is

* Complex
* Sensitive to quality or context
* Performed infrequently (so documentation alone won’t suffice)

### Typical scenarios

* onboarding a teammate
* transferring a client relationship
* teaching a specific workflow or [judgment call](https://www.google.com/search?q=judgment+call).

### How to run paired tasking

1. Schedule a focused session where both people work together on the task.
2. Observe and narrate: the person teaching explains decisions out loud.
3. Swap roles or let the learner take the lead partway through.
4. Compare results and explicitly discuss differences.
5. Repeat 3–4 iterations until the learner reliably produces acceptable results.
