# [Care rules](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=JUbwC0qg5O6RqypfwUiTj5)

* Handle with care — avoid extreme shocks and harsh treatment, which shorten the watch’s life
* No sharp objects when cleaning
* Avoid chemical cleaners and solvents like WD-40 — they can ruin the waterproof seals

---

# Troubleshooting Methods

* **Warm water soak:** submerge in warm (not hot) water for 10–30 minutes to dissolve salt or grit / rinse out debris
* **Vinegar wash:** soak in a 50/50 mix of _white vinegar_ and [tepid water](https://www.google.com/search?q=tepid+water) for a couple of hours, gently brush around the button with a soft toothbrush, wash with _dish soap_

	> You can use a jar with a lid, block the watch so it can’t rattle around, then shake the jar with the watch inside

* **Press & flush:** While submerged or under a low-pressure faucet, repeatedly press the Down button ~20 times to flush out trapped particles / wiggle the button under running water
* **Unstick by hand:** Rotate & pull out the button
* **[Lubrication](https://www.reddit.com/r/Garmin/s/vvcUWougZw):** Tiny drop of **silicone-based spray** in the button gaps with a toothpick, then work the button in and out
* **[Button recalibration](https://www.reddit.com/r/GarminWatches/s/PAHNAG32G3)** (software-level fix, not physical cleaning)
	1. Plug the watch into a computer that can read MTP devices (a third-party program [^1] may be needed for the Mac to see the watch as a drive)
	2. Locate the watch’s drive on the computer and open it
	3. Create an empty file named `KEY_CALIBRATION.BIN` in the Internal Storage — _it must not sit in a subfolder_
	4. Disconnect the watch from the computer

[^1]: Such as [Android File Transfer](https://android.p2hp.com/filetransfer/index.html) or [OpenMTP](https://openmtp.ganeshrvel.com/)
