**汗蒸幕 (hanjeungmak, 한증막)** is the traditional Korean-style [sauna](sauna.md). it’s a form of deliberate heat exposure / thermotherapy

* The term literally means “sweat-steaming curtain” — a heated room (能量屋) you sit in and sweat
* Modern places often add themed rooms:
	* salt room (鹽房)
	* ice room (冰房)
	* mineral/stone room (礦石房)
	* mugwort stone room (艾草石房)

# 碧璽汗蒸 (Tourmaline Hanjeungmak)

* **Not** a dry sauna (乾蒸), **not** a steam oven (蒸氣烤箱) — a separate category
* The room is built from [tourmaline (碧璽)](https://www.google.com/search?q=tourmaline) panels that emit **far-infrared rays (遠紅外線)** + **negative ions (負離子)**
* Constant low temperature of **38–43°C**

# 艾草石房 (Mugwort Stone Room)

* Featuring heated **mugwort stone**, which fills the air with the herbal aroma of [mugwort (艾草 / 쑥)](https://www.google.com/search?q=艾草)
* 溫熱＋香氣，給人安神、放鬆的感覺

# Benefits (as claimed by the venue)

* **Detox**: expels dampness (排濕), cold (排寒), and toxins (排毒)
* **Circulation**: unblocks meridians (經絡) and blood vessels (血管)
* **Cellular**: activates & repairs cells; wakes body cells from a “dormant” to an “active” state (細胞浴); calms the mind (安神); improves sleep & insomnia
* **Body**: balances body pH; beauty (美容); weight loss (減肥); reduces water retention
* **Systems**: improves blood circulation & respiratory function; relieves joint pain & inflammation/swelling; supports digestion and kidney function
* **Immunity**: strengthens immunity; helps prevent colds

# 為何汗蒸後不能馬上洗澡？

汗蒸結束後，體溫仍高、皮膚血管仍擴張，散熱還沒結束，汗腺會繼續工作 — 這就是 **二次出汗**。若此時立刻沖冷水會使血管急速收縮、散熱中斷，容易頭痛、感冒，也讓汗蒸效果大打折扣。

**整健反應**，指在進行汗蒸療程後，身體在短暫適應或修復過程中，出現的一些暫時性不適現象。
