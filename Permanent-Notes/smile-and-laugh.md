# Practices: how to smile and laugh more

* **Set the face for the day** — smile at yourself in the mirror first thing in the morning.
* **Laugh out loud, not just inwardly** — pretend-laughing for ten seconds usually becomes real laughing. The body doesn’t fully know the difference.
* **Ask “what’s the joke here?”** — when something goes wrong, hunt for the absurd angle. There is almost always one.
* **Keep levity in your media diet** — consume genuinely funny things (e.g. memes) on purpose, like a nutrient. [^2]
* **Laugh with people, not at them** — shared laughter builds bonds; laughter at someone’s expense breaks them.

[^1]: 自貶、自嘲、自我揭短
[^2]: Don’t underestimate the power of **memes (aka Pebbling)**. It signals that you’re thinking of them and want them to share your joy. It’s an act of care. Every pebble is a bid for (social) connection.
