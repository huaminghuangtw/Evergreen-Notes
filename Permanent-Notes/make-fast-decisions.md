> “It’s better to be boldly decisive and risk being wrong than to agonise at length and be right too late.” — Marilyn Moats Kennedy

---

Make fast (_but not rushed_) decisions as quickly as possible. Any decision is better than no decision.

* [If you aren’t clear on where you want to go, any road will take you there.](https://www.sahilbloom.com/newsletter/the-cheshire-cat-principle-how-to-create-clarity)
* [It’s important to do things & make decisions very fast!](https://nat.org)
	* You learn more per unit time because you make contact with reality more frequently.
	* Going fast makes you focus on what’s important.

---

By **[Patrick](https://patrickcollison.com/fast) [Collison](https://www.grahammann.net/blog/patrick-collison-on-decision-making)**

> “I now just place more value on decision speed. <mark>If you can make twice as many decisions at half the precision, that’s actually often better.</mark>”

> “<mark>Make more decisions with less confidence but in significantly less time.</mark> And just recognize that in most cases, you can course correct and treat fast decisions as a kind of asset and capability in their own right.”

> “You don’t necessarily need to be that good at decision making if you get really good at remaking the decision when and as necessary…Instead, <mark>it’s all about the constant feedback and course and error correction.</mark> And I think that’s, as a general matter, a better model for life and that even a lot of the decisions that look pretty trapdoor may not necessarily be.”
