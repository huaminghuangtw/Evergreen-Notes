= 這也會過去

![](https://media.huam.ing/image/26886d59a312b0cfb0494c41ce69ae82.webp)

“This too shall pass” is a phrase rooted in ancient wisdom. It reminds us of the impermanence of all things—joy and sorrow, success and failure, pleasure and pain. No state, however intense, is permanent. Everything is fleeting.

---

世事無常，人生的起起伏伏都會過去，無論是美好的事物或痛苦的經歷。
